Delete Account

You can request deletion of your Liva account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

If you can sign in to Liva, use the account-deletion option available from your account/profile settings. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app. Once the request is accepted, access to the account is disabled and deletion is processed through Liva's account-deletion workflow.

If you cannot access the app

Email livavoula2026@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Liva account and state that you want the account deleted. Do not send your password, authentication codes, or other secrets.

We may ask for limited information necessary to verify that the request relates to your account before acting on it.

What deletion covers

The deletion process is intended to remove or deactivate the Liva account and associated personal data that is no longer required for the service, including account and booking-related information, subject to the technical deletion workflow and any lawful retention described in the Privacy Policy.

Some limited records may be retained where there is a lawful reason, such as compliance with a legal obligation, security or integrity needs, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Data is not retained merely because it might be useful later.
