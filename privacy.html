Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Liva mobile application handles personal information used to provide Pilates class booking and studio services.

Information we process

Depending on how you use Liva, the app may process:

  • account information such as your name and email address;
  • an optional phone number if you choose to provide one;
  • booking, cancellation and waitlist information;
  • device notification registration information required to deliver push notifications;
  • basic operational information needed to authenticate your account and keep the service secure.

How we use information

We use this information to operate the Liva service, including account authentication, displaying the studio schedule, managing bookings and waitlists, showing upcoming bookings, supporting studio administration, sending service-related notifications, and processing account-deletion requests.

Who can access your information

Studio administrators may access information that is necessary to operate classes, such as the names and contact details of clients booked into a class. Liva also relies on service providers that support authentication, hosting, database operations, email and push notifications. These services are used only as needed to operate the app.

Data retention and account deletion

You can request deletion of your Liva account from inside the app. Once a deletion request is accepted, the account is scheduled for deletion and access to the account is disabled. Some limited records may be retained where necessary for security, integrity, legal obligations, or to preserve the consistency of booking history.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and booking information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

Liva is intended for use in connection with Pilates studio services and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service changes. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, please contact Liva Pilates Studio using the support contact published on this website and in the App Store listing.
